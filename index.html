About

Laura is a product design leader who (still) believes in leading with empathy, working with humans (and agents) at the intersection of strategy, design systems, platform tools, and agentic workflows.

She has led design and strategy across newsrooms, platforms, and product spaces, crafting user experiences and goal-oriented tooling from B2B banking systems to global editorial products.
